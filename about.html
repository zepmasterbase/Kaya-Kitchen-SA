About Kaya Kitchen

Kaya Kitchen is a proudly African food brand based in Randfontein, bringing bold street flavours, home-style meals, and affordable comfort food to the West Rand community.

Our Story

We started Kaya Kitchen with one goal — to serve real African food that tastes like home. From pap & stew to jollof and grilled chicken, every meal is prepared fresh daily with love, flavour, and passion.
