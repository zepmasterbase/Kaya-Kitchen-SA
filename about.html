About Kaya Kitchen

Authentic South African flavour served fresh in the heart of Randfontein. From hearty local meals to street-style favourites, Kaya Kitchen brings people together through food.

Our Story

Kaya Kitchen was built with one mission — to serve delicious, affordable, and authentic meals that feel like home.

Located in Randfontein and proudly serving the West Rand region of South Africa, we specialize in flavour-packed African meals made with passion and consistency.

Whether you're craving pap & stew, spicy street chicken, or rich jollof rice, every plate is prepared with bold local flavour and quality ingredients.

We believe food should be affordable, satisfying, and unforgettable.
